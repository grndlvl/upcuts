## Why UpCuts May Fit grndlvl Launch

UpCuts appears to have several favorable signals:

- It is a new, locally owned business rather than a chain.
- Three working stylists suggest an operating business with recurring revenue.
- The owners have substantial industry experience even though the business itself is new.
- Its current `company.site` page is a basic hosted presence rather than a strong standalone brand destination.
- The salon has clear customer actions: get directions, call, review services, meet the stylists, or request an appointment.
- A stronger site could help the salon establish an identity separate from the founders' previous employer.
- The Aiken location expands the grndlvl Launch portfolio beyond Augusta and North Augusta.

Do not assume the owners dislike their current page or want to replace it. The outreach should present an opportunity, not criticize their choices.

## Qualification Tasks

### 1. Confirm the Business Is Active

Verify:

- Current operating status
- Address and hours
- Phone and email
- Names and roles of the owners or primary decision-makers
- Whether all three founders are still associated with the business
- Date the salon opened

Prefer first-party sources, current social posts, chamber records, and the business's Google Business Profile.

### 2. Inventory the Current Digital Presence

Find and record:

- Official Facebook page
- Official Instagram account
- Google Business Profile
- Apple Maps and Bing Places listings, if present
- Booking platform, if any
- Review profiles
- Any domain or standalone website not already identified
- Directory listings with inconsistent information

Confirm whether `upcuts.company.site` is still the primary website before describing UpCuts as lacking a standalone site.

### 3. Identify Evidence of Ability to Pay

Look for non-invasive public indicators such as:

- Number of active stylists
- Review count and recency
- Appointment availability or visible customer demand
- Service pricing
- Retail product sales
- Business memberships or ribbon-cutting participation
- Investment in signage, branding, equipment, or the physical location
- Consistent operating hours and recent social activity

Do not speculate about revenue, profitability, credit, or the owners' personal finances.

### 4. Identify Customer Friction

Evaluate the current customer journey on mobile and desktop. Determine whether a prospective customer can quickly answer:

- What services does UpCuts offer?
- What do services cost, or how can someone request pricing?
- Who are the stylists?
- Can a customer book an appointment?
- Are walk-ins accepted?
- Where is the salon located?
- What are today's hours?
- Is the salon appropriate for women, men, and children?
- Are discounts or special offers current?
- How can a customer call, message, or get directions?

Record concrete gaps. Avoid vague statements such as “the site looks outdated.”

### 5. Check Brand and Search Risks

Investigate:

- Whether “UpCuts” is easily confused with similarly named businesses
- Whether the salon ranks for searches such as `hair salon Aiken SC`, `haircut Aiken SC`, and `walk-in haircut Aiken`
- Whether search results correctly associate the business with Aiken
- Inconsistent names, addresses, phone numbers, or hours across listings
- Whether social profiles and directory listings link to the correct destination

## Potential Launch Scope

Prepare a proposed one-page site concept containing only features justified by the research. Likely sections include:

1. Clear value proposition and call-to-action
2. Services and starting-price guidance, if the owners publish or approve pricing
3. Stylist introductions
4. Walk-in and appointment information
5. Current hours and location
6. Military, first-responder, and student discounts
7. Customer reviews
8. Photo gallery
9. One-tap call, directions, and booking actions
10. Local SEO and structured business data

Possible future additions may include online booking, gift cards, stylist-specific schedules, retail products, email or SMS offers, and review-request automation. Do not promise these as part of the initial Launch build unless separately scoped.

## Outreach Positioning

Lead with the new-business milestone and the opportunity to establish an owned digital home. Do not lead with criticism of the existing page.

Suggested positioning:

> UpCuts already has the ingredients for a strong local brand: experienced stylists, a clear community focus, and a new Aiken location. A focused standalone site could give customers one polished place to meet the team, understand services, confirm walk-in and appointment options, and get directions.

Connect the offer to business outcomes:

- Establishing an identity independent of a generic hosted page
- Making local search information easier to understand
- Reducing repetitive phone and social-media questions
- Helping new customers choose a stylist and take the next step
- Creating a foundation that can grow with the salon

Prepare two drafts:

1. A short social direct message of no more than 90 words
2. A concise email of no more than 180 words

Each draft must:
